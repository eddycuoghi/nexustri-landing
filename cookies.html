Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how nexustri.com (the “website”), operated by NexusTri LLC, uses cookies and similar technologies. Necessary storage is used to remember your choices. Analytics cookies are optional and are only set after you consent.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device through your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, can store information in a comparable way. In this policy, “cookies” refers to both.

2. Cookies we use

Google Analytics is loaded only after you consent. It records page views and interactions such as scrolling or starting to fill in the contact form; the contents of the form fields are not sent to it. Advertising storage and ad personalization are kept disabled. This website does not use advertising or social-media tracking cookies. Its fonts and images are served from our own website, and the contact form does not set cookies.

3. Managing your choice

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page. If you withdraw consent, the website stops sending data to Google Analytics, including from other pages of this website you have open, and deletes the Google Analytics cookies. Data sent before you withdrew consent is not affected.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block local storage, the website cannot remember your choice.

4. More information

For how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy. For questions about cookies or privacy, contact legal@nexustri.com or use our contact page.
