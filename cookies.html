Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how nexustri.com (the “website”), operated by NexusTri LLC, uses cookies and similar technologies. Necessary storage is used to remember your choices. Analytics cookies are optional and are only set after you consent.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device through your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, can store information in a comparable way. In this policy, “cookies” refers to both.

2. Cookies we use

Google Analytics is loaded only after you consent. Advertising storage and ad personalization are kept disabled. This website does not use advertising or social-media tracking cookies, and its fonts are served from our own website.

3. Managing your choice

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page. If you withdraw consent, Google Analytics stops collecting data and its cookies on this website are deleted.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block local storage, the website cannot remember your choice.

4. More information

For how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy. For questions, contact support@nexustri.com.
