What we build

Software for decisions that are costly to get wrong

Running a business means making important calls with incomplete information. We build focused digital tools that bring the relevant facts together, so the decision in front of you is clearer before time and money are committed.

  • One decision, done well

    Each product answers one practical question instead of trying to do everything.

  • The answer first

    Results lead with a plain-language conclusion, followed by the evidence behind it.

  • Technology as a means

    We use automation and AI where they improve the analysis, and exact, repeatable logic where the numbers must add up.
