Products

Focused tools for specific decisions

Each NexusTri product is built around one decision that is costly to get wrong, and aims to answer it as clearly as possible.

NameProof

Live

Brand-name validation and decision support

Helps founders assess a business name before they build a brand around it, with a clear recommendation and the evidence behind it.

  • Preliminary name assessment and a full Brand Decision Report
  • Domain and social-handle availability signals
  • SEO collision analysis
  • Preliminary trademark screening signals across USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO and UK Companies House / UKIPO
  • Pronunciation and linguistic review

Decision support only. Not legal advice.

Visit nameproof.io (opens in a new tab)

MarginGuides

Public Beta

Profitability and margin decision support

Helps sellers see the real margin of a deal before committing to it, turning real costs, fees and a target margin into a clear profitability decision.

  • Real margin, real profit and the gap to a target margin
  • Break-even and target selling prices
  • Stress Test and Resilience views for adverse cost, fee and FX scenarios
  • Improve My Margin: what would need to change to reach the target
  • Free calculators for profit margin, break-even ROAS and landed cost

Currently in Public Beta.

Visit marginguides.com (opens in a new tab)

NameProof and MarginGuides each operate on their own website and are subject to that website’s terms and policies. Current features, availability and pricing are described on each product’s website.

Approach

How we build

Short. Direct. Serious. Our products are designed to make a decision clearer, not to add more noise around it.

  1. 01

    Decision first

    Each product starts from a concrete decision and leads with a clear answer, followed by the evidence behind it.

  2. 02

    AI where it helps

    We use AI where it improves analysis, and deterministic logic where results need to be exact and repeatable.

  3. 03

    Clear about limits

    Our tools support decisions. They do not replace professional legal, financial or tax advice, and we say so plainly.

  4. 04

    Honest product status

    We label what is live and what is in beta, and describe only what our products do today.
