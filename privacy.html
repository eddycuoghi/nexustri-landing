Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NexusTri LLC (“NexusTri”, “we”, “us”, “our”) handles personal data when you visit nexustri.com (the “website”) or contact us. We aim to collect only the information necessary to operate the website and to reply to you.

This policy covers this website only. NameProof (nameproof.io) and MarginGuides (marginguides.com) each have their own website and privacy information, which apply when you use those products.

1. Who is responsible

NexusTri LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, United States.

Privacy matters: legal@nexustri.com

Corporate Mailing Address (for official corporate correspondence):
400 Rella Blvd, #207-298, Montebello, NY 10901, United States

2. What data we process

  • Technical data. When you visit the website, our hosting provider (Netlify) processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time of the request. This is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure.
  • Your cookie choice. Your consent choice is stored in your browser’s local storage on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • Analytics data (only with your consent). If you accept analytics, Google Analytics collects information about how the website is used, such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and the referring website. It can also record interactions such as scrolling to the end of a page or starting to fill in the contact form (for example, a “form_start” event). The contents of the form fields are not sent to Google Analytics. We use this information in aggregated form.
  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, we process your name, your email address, the subject you choose and your message, in order to handle and reply to your inquiry. Submissions are received and stored through Netlify’s form handling service (Netlify Forms), and we may also receive them by email notification. Netlify automatically checks submissions for spam using the Akismet spam-filtering service. The form also contains a hidden anti-spam field that people do not see or fill in; submissions that complete it are rejected automatically. Contacting us is optional. If you choose to use the contact form, your name, email address, subject and message are needed so that we can understand and reply to your inquiry; without them the form cannot be sent. You can email us instead.
  • Messages you send us by email. If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply.

The website has no user accounts or payment features, and we do not ask you for sensitive data. Please do not include sensitive personal information, payment details or passwords in your messages.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Operating and securing the website, including spam protection for the contact form — our legitimate interests in providing a working, secure website.
  • Analytics — your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Replying to your messages and contact form submissions — our legitimate interests in responding to inquiries, or steps taken at your request before entering into an agreement.

4. Cookies

Analytics cookies are only set after you consent. Details about the cookies and similar technologies we use are in our Cookie Policy. You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page.

5. Service providers and data sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising. We share data only with providers that help us run the website:

  • Netlify — website hosting and delivery, and receipt, storage and spam filtering of contact form submissions. For spam filtering, Netlify uses Akismet.
  • Google — Google Analytics, only if you consent. Advertising storage and ad personalization are kept disabled.
  • Namecheap — our email hosting provider, to receive and answer messages sent to our nexustri.com addresses, including notifications of contact form submissions.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. International data transfers

NexusTri LLC is a United States company, and our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws may differ from those in your country. Where the data protection law applicable to the processing requires specific safeguards for such transfers, you can ask us which safeguards apply and request a copy of them by writing to legal@nexustri.com.

7. Data retention

  • Technical data is kept by our hosting provider only as long as needed for operation and security, according to its policies.
  • Analytics data is kept according to the data retention setting of our Google Analytics property. For standard properties, Google Analytics keeps event-level data for at most 14 months; this setting does not apply to aggregated reports.
  • Messages and contact form submissions, including copies held by Netlify and email notifications, are kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any follow-up, and are then deleted, unless we need to keep them longer to comply with a legal obligation or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you change or clear it. We ask again after 12 months.

8. Your rights

Depending on the data protection law applicable to the processing, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before the withdrawal. Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, you may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work or where you believe an infringement took place.

To exercise your rights, contact legal@nexustri.com.

9. Children

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last revised.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact legal@nexustri.com, or use our contact page and choose “Legal or privacy inquiry”. For general questions, contact support@nexustri.com.
