Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NexusTri LLC (“NexusTri”, “we”, “us”, “our”) handles personal data when you visit nexustri.com (the “website”). We aim to collect only the information necessary to operate the website.

This policy covers this website only. NameProof (nameproof.io) and MarginGuides (marginguides.com) each have their own website and privacy information, which apply when you use those products.

1. Who is responsible

NexusTri LLC, a limited liability company organized in Wyoming, United States.

Email: support@nexustri.com

Corporate mailing address (postal correspondence only, not an operating office):
400 Rella Blvd, #207-298, Montebello, NY 10901, United States

2. What data we process

  • Technical data. When you visit the website, our hosting provider (Netlify) processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time of the request. This is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure.
  • Your cookie choice. Your consent choice is stored in your browser’s local storage on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • Analytics data (only with your consent). If you accept analytics, Google Analytics collects information about how the website is used, such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and the referring website. We use this information in aggregated form.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply.

The website has no user accounts, contact forms or payment features, and we do not ask you for sensitive data.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Operating and securing the website — our legitimate interests in providing a working, secure website.
  • Analytics — your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Replying to your messages — our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, or steps taken at your request before entering into an agreement.

4. Cookies

Analytics cookies are only set after you consent. Details about the cookies and similar technologies we use are in our Cookie Policy. You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page.

5. Service providers and data sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising. We share data only with providers that help us run the website:

  • Netlify — website hosting and delivery.
  • Google — Google Analytics, only if you consent. Advertising storage and ad personalization are kept disabled.
  • Our email service provider — to receive and answer messages sent to us.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. International data transfers

NexusTri LLC is a United States company, and our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

7. Data retention

  • Technical data is kept by our hosting provider only as long as needed for operation and security, according to its policies.
  • Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in Google Analytics (for standard Google Analytics 4 properties, at most 14 months for event-level data) and is used in aggregated form.
  • Messages are kept as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any follow-up, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you change or clear it. We ask again after 12 months.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, contact support@nexustri.com.

9. Children

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last revised.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact support@nexustri.com.
